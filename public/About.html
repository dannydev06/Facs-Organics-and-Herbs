For over a decade, FACS Herbs has been a trusted source for premium, organic herbs, spices, and natural products, dedicated to enhancing your health and well-being.

From a Passion to a Purpose

FACS Herbs began with a simple belief: that everyone deserves access to pure, potent, and affordable natural products. What started as a small family endeavor, sourcing the finest herbs for our own kitchen, blossomed into a mission to share nature's healing power with our community.

Founded in 2010 in Lagos, Nigeria, we've grown from a local stall to a trusted online source, but our core values remain unchanged. We are still a family-run business, personally curating every product that bears our name.
